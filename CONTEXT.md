# Erlebnishof Visitor Experience

This context defines the shared language for welcoming guests, describing a changing food and farm-shop offering, and publishing short-lived information for Erlebnishof Auszeit.

## Language

### Place and guests

**Erlebnishof Auszeit**:
The whole informal, family-oriented venue, including the Hofcafé, Hofladen, and farm setting.
_Avoid_: Using Hofcafé or Restaurant as a synonym for the whole venue

**Gast (Guest)**:
A person, family, or group considering or making a visit to Erlebnishof Auszeit.
_Avoid_: User, lead, customer account

**Gästesprache (Guest Voice)**:
Friendly, clear German addressing guests consistently as **„ihr/euch“**. It feels warm and informal without relying on dialect or insider language.
_Avoid_: Sie/Ihnen, mixed forms of address, corporate marketing language

**Hofcafé**:
The food-and-drink part of Erlebnishof Auszeit, focused on coffee, cakes, Brotzeit, and lunch.
_Avoid_: Restaurant, Speiselokal

**Hofcafé-Besuch (Hofcafé Visit)**:
An informal stay during Hofcafé opening hours. A table may be requested in advance, but a reservation is not required for every visit.
_Avoid_: Appointment, mandatory booking

**Hofladen**:
The on-site farm shop with a changing range of products and opening hours independent of the Hofcafé.
_Avoid_: Online shop, treating it as part of the café schedule

**Hofladen-Einkauf (Farm-shop Visit)**:
A visit to buy from the Hofladen during its own opening hours. It does not imply that Hofcafé staff or all farm facilities are available.
_Avoid_: Hofcafé-Besuch

**Selbstbedienungs-Hofladen (Self-service Farm Shop)**:
The Hofladen is operated as self-service throughout its stated opening hours.
_Avoid_: Implying that café service is available whenever the shop is open

**Spontanbesuch (Spontaneous Visit)**:
A visit during the applicable opening hours without a prior arrangement. It does not guarantee a particular table or service outside the relevant area’s hours.
_Avoid_: Walk-in guarantee, mandatory reservation

**Tischreservierungsanfrage (Table-reservation Request)**:
A request that still requires confirmation by the team.
_Avoid_: Confirmed reservation

**Tischreservierung (Table Reservation)**:
A table arrangement that the team has confirmed directly with the guest.
_Avoid_: Unconfirmed enquiry

**Gruppenanfrage (Group Enquiry)**:
A discussion about a possible celebration or group visit that remains unconfirmed until the team agrees the details.
_Avoid_: Event booking, confirmed package

**Feiern & Gruppen (Celebrations and Groups)**:
Birthdays, family celebrations, and small company or team groups discussed individually with the team.
_Avoid_: Private Feiern & Events, fixed package

**Kontakt-E-Mail-Adresse (Contact Email Address)**:
The public email address **hallo@erlebnishof-auszeit.de** for reservations, group enquiries, and general contact.
_Avoid_: Destination mailbox, form address

**Haupttelefonnummer (Primary Telephone Number)**:
The Festnetz number used as the main public telephone contact. The mobile number is secondary contact information.
_Avoid_: Treating both numbers as equally primary

**Gut zu wissen (Good to Know)**:
Owner-verified visit facts about parking, payment, accessibility features, high chairs, changing facilities, dogs, children, and indoor or outdoor seating.
_Avoid_: Unqualified „barrierefrei“ claims, unsupported amenity promises

### Food and products

**Hofcafé-Angebot (Hofcafé Offering)**:
The changing selection of coffee, cakes, Brotzeit, and lunch. Individual dishes and prices are not stable website information.
_Avoid_: Fixed menu, product catalogue

**Aktuelle Speisekarte (Current Menu)**:
The most recently maintained list of the Hofcafé’s changing food and drink offering.
_Avoid_: Permanent menu, advertised price promise

**Kuchenauswahl (Cake Selection)**:
The changing selection of cakes, which is mostly made in house without claiming that every cake is homemade.
_Avoid_: „Alle Kuchen hausgemacht“, fixed weekly list

**Regional bezogen (Locally Sourced)**:
Food sourced mostly from nearby producers or suppliers; it does not mean that every ingredient is local.
_Avoid_: Exclusively local, entirely farm-produced

**Hofladen-Sortiment (Farm-shop Range)**:
The changing products available in the Hofladen. Named products are non-binding examples introduced with **„Je nach Saison und Verfügbarkeit“**.
_Avoid_: Fixed stock, price catalogue, unsupported „direkt vom Hof“ claim

### Opening and calendar language

**Ortszeit (Local Time)**:
The Europe/Berlin time used when interpreting all opening, event, editorial, and seasonal boundaries.
_Avoid_: Browser-local time, UTC

**Lokaler Kalendertag (Local Calendar Day)**:
A date from midnight through the following midnight in Ortszeit, whether or not Hofcafé or Hofladen is open.
_Avoid_: Betriebstag

**Reguläre Öffnungszeiten (Regular Opening Hours)**:
The stable schedules: Hofcafé Friday through Sunday from 08:30–17:00; self-service Hofladen daily from 08:00–19:00 in September through March and 08:00–21:00 in April through August.
_Avoid_: Combining both schedules, treating a temporary closure as regular hours

**Abweichende Öffnungszeiten (Opening-hours Exception)**:
A temporary closure or replacement schedule for Hofcafé, Hofladen, or both on a specific date or bounded date range.
_Avoid_: Neuigkeit, permanent schedule change

**Bevorstehende Abweichung (Upcoming Exception)**:
An announced opening-hours exception shown before it takes effect so guests can plan ahead.
_Avoid_: Draft exception, current opening status

**Aktuell geltende Abweichung (Current Exception)**:
The opening-hours exception, if any, that changes the applicable regular schedule now.
_Avoid_: Upcoming exception, permanent hours

**Aktueller Hofcafé-Öffnungsstatus (Current Hofcafé Status)**:
Whether the Hofcafé is open at the present Ortszeit according to its regular hours and any currently applicable exception.
_Avoid_: Whole-venue status, occupancy or table-availability claim

**Aktueller Hofladen-Öffnungsstatus (Current Hofladen Status)**:
Whether the self-service Hofladen is open at the present Ortszeit according to its seasonal regular hours and any currently applicable exception.
_Avoid_: Whole-venue status, implying café service

### Current information

**Aktuelles & Termine (News and Dates)**:
The homepage collection of short-lived Neuigkeiten and ongoing or upcoming Öffentliche Termine published by the editorial team.
_Avoid_: Blog, news feed, general event calendar

**Neuigkeit (Update)**:
A short piece of current information about Erlebnishof Auszeit.
_Avoid_: Article, post

**Öffentlicher Termin (Public Date)**:
An occasion that any guest may attend.
_Avoid_: Private Feier, booking

**Terminzeitraum (Event Period)**:
The start and optional end of an Öffentlicher Termin in Ortszeit. Without a stated end, the Termin remains current through the end of its start day.
_Avoid_: File date, Freigabezeitpunkt

**Beitragsbild (Entry Image)**:
The single optional flyer or photograph that supplements a Neuigkeit or Öffentlicher Termin without replacing its meaningful text.
_Avoid_: Image-only announcement, gallery

**Anzeigezeitraum (Display Window)**:
The interval in which a Neuigkeit is intended to be current, using its planned first day and optional expiry day. An Öffentlicher Termin is current according to its Terminzeitraum.
_Avoid_: File creation time, upload time

**Freigabezeitpunkt (Approval Time)**:
The moment the editorial team approves an entry for future or immediate publication.
_Avoid_: Upload time, event start, first public appearance

**Veröffentlichungszeitpunkt (Publication Time)**:
The moment an approved entry first becomes visible to guests.
_Avoid_: Freigabezeitpunkt, event start

**Entwurf (Draft)**:
An editorial entry still being prepared and not approved for publication.
_Avoid_: Published-but-hidden entry

**Geplant (Scheduled)**:
An approved entry whose intended public period has not begun.
_Avoid_: Draft, currently visible entry

**Veröffentlicht (Published)**:
An approved entry currently visible to guests.
_Avoid_: Draft, planned entry

**Abgelaufen (Expired)**:
An entry retained after its public period has ended but no longer shown to guests.
_Avoid_: Deleted

**Archiviert (Archived)**:
An entry deliberately retained outside the current collection.
_Avoid_: Papierkorb, published entry

**Papierkorb (Trash)**:
Editorial records and media marked for recoverable removal before permanent deletion.
_Avoid_: Immediate permanent deletion

**Vorschau (Preview)**:
The private representation the editorial team checks before publication.
_Avoid_: Public hidden page, approval queue

**Neu-Hinweis (New-content Indicator)**:
An indication that current information has appeared or materially changed since the guest explicitly marked it as read on that device.
_Avoid_: Push notification, marker for theme or opening-hours changes

**Redaktionsbereich (Editorial Admin)**:
The private area for maintaining Aktuelles & Termine, Abweichende Öffnungszeiten, and seasonal-theme timing.
_Avoid_: General-purpose CMS, website builder

**Bearbeitungsberechtigte (Authorized Editors)**:
The two people permitted to maintain the Redaktionsbereich.
_Avoid_: Public users, subscribers

### Presentation

**Markencharakter (Brand Character)**:
Warm, family-oriented, rural, and playfully polished, using authentic photography and the venue’s whimsical animal logo. Wheat, burlap styling, and generic Hofladen clichés are explicitly excluded.
_Avoid_: Wheat motif, generic rustic template, formal restaurant styling

**Markenlogo (Brand Logo)**:
The existing whimsical animal-and-lettering artwork.
_Avoid_: Generic farm emblem, wheat motif

**Saisonthema (Seasonal Theme)**:
A temporary visual atmosphere for Christmas, spring, summer, autumn, or winter that does not change the business offering.
_Avoid_: Promotion, event, permanent redesign

**Themenfenster (Theme Window)**:
Fourteen consecutive local calendar days selected by the editorial team for Spring, Summer, or Autumn; the first day counts as day one.
_Avoid_: Fifteen-day inclusive range, permanent theme

**Themenmodus (Theme Mode)**:
The editorial choice between **Automatisch**, **Aus**, and a Manuelle Themenwahl.
_Avoid_: Treating Automatisch or Aus as themes

**Manuelle Themenwahl (Manual Theme Choice)**:
A named Saisonthema selected independently of automatic calendar windows.
_Avoid_: Automatic calendar result, guest preference

**Aktives Saisonthema (Active Seasonal Theme)**:
The single theme currently resolved from the Themenmodus; calendar windows constrain only Automatisch.
_Avoid_: All configured themes, motion preference

**Saisoneffekt (Seasonal Effect)**:
Optional decorative movement associated with an Aktives Saisonthema.
_Avoid_: Business content, required interaction

**Bewegungspräferenz (Motion Preference)**:
The guest’s choice whether optional visual movement is shown.
_Avoid_: Themenmodus, separate per-effect preferences

### External presence

**Google-Unternehmensprofil (Google Business Profile)**:
The owner-maintained Google listing carrying the current menu, business hours, special hours, and map presence.
_Avoid_: Strukturierte Betriebsdaten, website admin

**Strukturierte Betriebsdaten (Structured Business Data)**:
The website’s machine-readable representation of the Hofcafé and Hofladen as separate but related parts of Erlebnishof Auszeit.
_Avoid_: Google-Unternehmensprofil, Restaurant, one combined opening schedule
