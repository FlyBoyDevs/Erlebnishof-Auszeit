# Require legal sign-off before publication

Publication of the redesigned site is blocked until the final Impressum and Datenschutz receive Rechtliche Freigabe. The review occurs after IONOS hosting, GoatCounter, outbound social links, admin processing, storage, and every production network request are documented. Automated and manual technical checks then verify that the deployed behaviour matches the approved statements. Repository review and text generation are not treated as legal certification.
