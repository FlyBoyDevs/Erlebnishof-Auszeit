# Use a single-page visitor experience with separate legal pages

The public visitor experience will remain one mobile-first scrolling page because the offering is compact and guests should be able to explore it easily. Impressum and Datenschutz will be standalone, directly linkable pages so legal information remains reliably accessible independently of the main interface. Öffentliche Termine remain readable homepage cards; the site does not create per-event leaf pages or claim Google Event rich-result eligibility in the current scope.
