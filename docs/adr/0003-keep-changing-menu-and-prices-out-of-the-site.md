# Keep the changing menu and prices out of the site

Individual dishes and prices change too frequently to duplicate reliably on the website. Google Business Profile/Maps remains the owner-maintained source for the current menu. The website replaces the existing Speisekarte presentation with **„Unser Hofcafé“**: a qualitative introduction to coffee, the mostly house-made cake selection, Brotzeit, lunch, and mostly locally sourced food; a manually controlled food-and-atmosphere Bilderkarussell; and an explicit Google-Maps-Verweis to the current menu.
